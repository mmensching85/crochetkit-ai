Where to find info on your label

Weight category — Look for the CYC number (0-7) or a symbol on the label band. Usually near the yarn name.

Yardage — Listed as "yards" or "yds" on the label, typically near the weight/grams.

Hook size — A crochet hook symbol with mm size is shown on most labels.
