About CrochetKit AI

Welcome to CrochetKit AI, your personal assistant for finding the perfect crochet project based on the materials you already own! Our mission is to inspire creativity and reduce yarn waste by connecting crafters with projects they can start right away, without needing to buy more supplies.

Our Vision

We believe that creativity should be accessible and sustainable. CrochetKit AI was born out of a desire to empower crocheters of all skill levels to make the most of their existing yarn stash. Instead of searching for patterns and then buying specific yarns, we flip the script: tell us what you have, and we'll show you what you can make!

How It Works

Our intelligent matching algorithm analyzes your available yarn (weight, yardage) and hook sizes, then recommends patterns that perfectly fit your materials. We focus on providing curated, high-quality suggestions that are genuinely achievable with your current stash.

  • Smart Matching: Input your yarn details, and our AI finds the best pattern matches.
  • Stash Management: Keep track of all your yarns and hooks in one convenient place.
  • Inspiration: Discover new projects and expand your crochet horizons.
  • Beginner-Friendly: We provide tips, common mistakes, and clear instructions to help new crocheters succeed.

Our Story

CrochetKit AI was developed by a passionate team of developers and crafters who understand the joy (and sometimes, the overwhelm) of a growing yarn stash. We're constantly working to improve our matching engine, expand our pattern library, and enhance the user experience.
